Added `~stixpy.calibration.energy.apply_elut_correction`, a spectral-index-weighted ELUT correction for STIX pixel data ported from ``stx_elut_correction.pro`` (P. Massa). The correction redistributes counts from the per-pixel ELUT energy intervals to the nominal science intervals under a local power-law spectrum assumption, handling three cases: single bin off the spectral peak, single bin at the peak (split power law), and multi-bin ranges (boundary corrections on the first and last bin). Result is returned as a new typed dataclass `~stixpy.calibration.energy.ELUTCorrectedCounts`. The spectral-index estimator (`~stixpy.calibration.energy.estimate_spectral_index`) is currently a stub; callers must supply ``sp_index`` and ``idx_peak`` until the IDL port lands as a follow-up.
